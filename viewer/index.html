Mouse and keyboard
  • Click or drag in a view to move all three.
  • Scroll, or ↑ ↓, steps through slices. Page Up and Page Down step ten.
  • Shift and an arrow key moves the crosshair within the slice.
  • M switches between FA and MD.
  • T shows or hides the tracts. O switches outline and fill.
  • [ and ] go to the previous and next tract.
  • , and . open the previous and next scan.
